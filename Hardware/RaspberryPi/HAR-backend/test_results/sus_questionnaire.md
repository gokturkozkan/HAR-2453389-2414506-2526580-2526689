## SUS-Style Scoring

Use the 10 scored answers above.

| Item Type | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Positive items: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 | Score contribution = response - 1 |
| Negative items: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 | Score contribution = 5 - response |
| Final score | Sum all 10 contributions and multiply by 2.5 |

The final score is between 0 and 100.

## Score Interpretation

| SUS Score Range | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| 0-50 | Poor usability |
| 51-67 | Below average usability |
| 68 | Average usability benchmark |
| 69-80 | Good usability |
| 81-100 | Excellent usability |
